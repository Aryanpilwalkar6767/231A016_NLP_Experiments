## Brief Theory

### TF-IDF

TF-IDF is a numerical technique used to represent the importance of words in a collection of documents.

It consists of two main components:

### Term Frequency (TF)

Term Frequency measures how frequently a word occurs in a document.

A word that occurs frequently in a document receives a higher TF value.

### Inverse Document Frequency (IDF)

Inverse Document Frequency reduces the importance of words that occur in many documents.

Words that appear in fewer documents receive higher importance.

### TF-IDF

TF-IDF combines TF and IDF to assign a numerical weight to each word.

The general idea is:

```text
TF-IDF = Term Frequency × Inverse Document Frequency
````

---

## Cosine Similarity

Cosine Similarity measures the similarity between two numerical vectors.

It calculates the cosine of the angle between two vectors.

The value generally ranges from:

```text
0 → Completely different
1 → Highly similar
```

For text documents, cosine similarity can be used to determine how similar their word representations are.
